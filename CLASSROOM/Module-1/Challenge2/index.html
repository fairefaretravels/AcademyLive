Most people treat every post like it needs a brand-new idea. That's why they run out of things to say by Wednesday.
One good idea, broken apart correctly, can feed a month of content.

📉 The Scratch Method

New idea for every post. Runs dry fast. Posting becomes a chore you dread.

VS

📈 The Multiplier Method

One idea, extracted into insights, reformatted into 30 pieces. Posting becomes assembly, not invention.
